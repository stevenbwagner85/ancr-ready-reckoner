Analytical Confidence Rating (AnCR) ready reckoner

A structured aid for judging analytical confidence. Every rating is the analyst’s judgement, not a calculation.

Assessment details

How to use this tool
  1. Enter each key judgement on its own tab. You can add up to ten.
  2. For each factor, move the slider between the two anchor statements. It moves in steps of 10 from 0 to 100. Record your reasoning in the notes.
  3. Set the overall slider for each criterion, and the overall confidence, yourself. They are judgements, not averages. A score of 0 to 30 reads as Low, 40 to 60 as Moderate and 70 to 100 as High.
  4. Use the ingredients and the writing frame at the foot of each tab to write the AnCR statement.
  5. Choose Save as Excel to keep a workbook on your own drive. Open workbook reloads one, including after it has been edited in Excel.

This file works offline and sends nothing anywhere. Your work is kept in this browser on this computer until you choose Start again.
